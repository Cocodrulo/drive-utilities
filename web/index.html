Google Workspace Add-on

Effortless Google Drive File & Folder Duplication

A secure, lightweight Workspace Add-on by Cocodrulo that allows you to easily duplicate, batch rename, and filter files directly inside your Google Drive.

Get Started Explore Features

Powerful Features

Discover the utilities designed to make your Google Drive file management more productive.

Duplicate Items

Seamlessly copy files and complex folder trees to any location in your Google Drive while preserving structure.

Advanced Renaming

Bulk customize copied files by adding prefixes, suffixes, or searching and replacing text in filenames.

Conflict Resolution

Choose exactly how to handle name collisions on copy operations: rename, overwrite, or skip files.

Flexible Filters

Refine your selection. Copy only files, only folders, or filter for specific Google Workspace formats.

Keep Permissions

Optionally carry over original viewer and editor access permissions to duplicated documents.

Simulation Mode

Run a virtual "dry run" simulation of your copy process to preview results before modifying Drive files.

Favorites & Recents

Save favorite target folders or quickly choose from a history of recent copy destinations.

Operation Logs

Track history. Review a complete and detailed log of your past copy and renaming processes.

100% Private & Secure

We do not store or transmit your data to any external servers. Operations execute safely within Google Workspace.
