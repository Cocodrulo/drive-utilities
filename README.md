# Drive Utilities

A simple Google Workspace Add-on with handy utilities to make your experience more comfortable. It allows you to easily select files or folders in your Google Drive and duplicate them to a destination of your choice.

## Features

- **Duplicate Files and Folders:** Easily copy your items to another location in your Drive.
- **Advanced Renaming:** Prepend prefixes, suffixes, or search and replace text in copied files.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Choose how to handle filename collisions (Rename, Overwrite, or Skip).
- **File Filtering:** Copy only files, only folders, or Google Workspace files.
- **Sharing Permissions:** Optionally preserve the original viewing and editing permissions on copied files.
- **Simulation Mode (Dry Run):** Run a simulation of the copy process to preview what would happen without altering Google Drive.
- **Favorites & Recents:** Save favorite destination folders or quickly pick from recent destinations.
- **History Log:** View the history of your recent copy operations.
- **Secure and Private:** We do not collect or store any of your files or personal information on external servers.
