# Drive Utilities Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

This application was developed as a simple add-on for Google Workspace using Google Apps Script.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, files, or user data on external servers.

## Use of Information

The requested access to Google Drive is used solely and exclusively to perform the app's main function: allowing the user to select their own files or folders and duplicate them to a destination of their choice.

## Local Storage

The identifiers of the selected folders are temporarily saved within the user's secure Google environment (PropertiesService) to maintain interface configuration, and are not accessible by third parties or the developer.

## Changes

If this policy changes, it will be updated in this same document. For inquiries, contact **[contact@cocodrulo.dev](mailto:contact@cocodrulo.dev)**.
